# ThermoSight — Research Index

This index documents the research resources reviewed during the design and development of **ThermoSight**.

The selected resources were examined to understand challenges and considerations related to extreme heatwaves, human thermal stress, urban heat, heat-health warning systems, heatwave prediction, urban cooling, heat-related mortality, Indian heatwave impacts, and Heat Action Plans.

# Research-to-Feature Overview

The research collection was used as a supporting source of evidence and context during the design of ThermoSight.

The relationship between the literature and the system is summarized below.

## 1. Heatwaves & Early Warning

**Research insight:**  

Heat-health warning systems can connect extreme heat conditions with potential health impacts and preparedness measures.

**ThermoSight design response:**  

The Early Warning Engine combines heat intelligence, forecast information and human-risk indicators to support early alerts and preparedness workflows.

---

## 2. Human Thermal Stress

**Research insight:**  

Human thermal conditions can be assessed using thermal indices that incorporate environmental conditions rather than relying only on air temperature.

**ThermoSight design response:**  

The Human Thermal Stress Engine incorporates thermal indicators such as UTCI and other heat-stress measures to characterize human heat exposure.

---

## 3. Urban Heat & Remote Sensing

**Research insight:**  

Remote sensing and spatial urban classifications can be used to analyse surface thermal patterns and identify areas affected by urban heat.

**ThermoSight design response:**  

City Intelligence and Heat Intelligence use spatial thermal information to identify heat-prone areas and understand urban heat patterns.

---

## 4. Heat-Health Risk

**Research insight:**  

Extreme heat can be associated with adverse health outcomes, including increased mortality, with effects varying by population and location.

**ThermoSight design response:**  

Public Health Intelligence incorporates population vulnerability and modelled health-risk indicators to provide a broader understanding of heat impacts.

---

## 5. AI/ML-Based Prediction

**Research insight:**  

Machine-learning approaches can use environmental and meteorological predictors to model and forecast heatwave conditions.

**ThermoSight design response:**  

The AI & Prediction Lab supports predictive modelling and multi-day heatwave forecasting using environmental and temporal data.

---

## 6. Urban Cooling & Mitigation

**Research insight:**  

Urban vegetation and tree canopy can contribute to local cooling, with effectiveness influenced by environmental and spatial factors.

**ThermoSight design response:**  

Intervention & Optimization evaluates potential cooling interventions and supports planning of urban heat mitigation strategies.

---

## 7. Indian Heatwave Context

**Research insight:**  

India experiences significant heatwave exposure, with regional differences in temperature patterns, climate projections and heat impacts.

**ThermoSight design response:**  

The platform incorporates India-specific heat intelligence and supports city-level analysis, forecasting and risk assessment.

---

## 8. Heat Action Plans & Governance

**Research insight:**  

Effective heat response requires coordination across multiple sectors and integration of heat-health information into preparedness and response planning.

**ThermoSight design response:**  

Government Decision Studio and Response & Heat Action provide decision-support workflows for planning, coordination and heat-response measures.

---

## 9. Mortality & Population Health

**Research insight:**  

Research from Indian cities demonstrates associations between extreme heat events and mortality outcomes.

**ThermoSight design response:**  

Mortality Risk and Public Health Intelligence provide modelled population-level risk indicators to support proactive heat-health planning.

# Research Use Disclaimer

The resources in this repository provide supporting scientific literature and contextual evidence for the research and design of ThermoSight.

They are included to document the scientific concepts, environmental considerations, health impacts and technical areas examined during development.

They should **not** be interpreted as validation of the ThermoSight system, its predictions, risk estimates, models or performance.
