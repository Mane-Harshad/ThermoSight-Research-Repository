# ThermoSight — Research Repository

This repository contains the research resources and supporting literature used during the design and development of **ThermoSight**, an AI-powered platform focused on extreme heatwave early warning and human thermal risk intelligence.

## Research Focus

The research collection examines key areas relevant to heatwave monitoring, thermal risk assessment and heat-health preparedness:

- Extreme heatwaves and their impacts
- Human thermal stress and thermal indices
- Urban heat and remote sensing
- Heat-health warning systems
- Heatwave prediction and AI/ML
- Urban cooling and heat mitigation
- Heat-related health and mortality
- Indian heatwaves and climate impacts
- Heat Action Plans and public-health preparedness

## Research Collection

The repository contains selected research papers and scientific resources reviewed to understand extreme heat, thermal stress, health impacts and heat-health preparedness, and to inform the design of ThermoSight.

They do not constitute validation of ThermoSight's predictions, risk estimates, models or system performance.
